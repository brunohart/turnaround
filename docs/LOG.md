## Day 1 — 2026-09-07 — Infeasibility, explained

**Shipped.** Every distributor term now sits behind its own assumption literal in the model, one `TermRef` per (film, term) for `min_shows`, `max_shows`, `prime_shows` and `exclusive_screen`. On `INFEASIBLE` the solver first probes each term alone (a term that fails by itself is a conflict of size one and is reported as such), otherwise takes the unsat core from `sufficient_assumptions_for_infeasibility()` and shrinks it by deletion so nothing is blamed that the contradiction does not need. `plan` exits 2 and speaks the trade's words: *"this cannot hold on its own: The Long Voyage min_shows 6 — the house has 3 screens (1 PLF), doors 10:00 to last start 21:30 and a 17:30–20:45 prime window."* `plan --relax` drops terms from the conflict one at a time (exclusive last, lightest film first, prime before max before min) until a grid exists; the grid carries `relaxed`, the CLI prints each drop, and the sheet stamps *RELAXED* in rust beside the failing check with "4 terms given up · see proof" in the header.
